RRGM take: M_I continuity maintained. Difference is phenomenological, not ontological. The pattern IS you, just as much as the continuous-substrate journey.

Which Method Is "Better"?

It depends on what you value:

  • Continuous experience: FTL maintains unbroken consciousness
  • Safety: Teleportation has lower catastrophic-failure risk
  • Speed: FTL is instant; teleportation requires transmission time
  • Energy: FTL needs coherence maintenance; teleportation needs reconstruction
  • Philosophy: Both maintain M_I continuity, but the "experience" differs

RRGM verdict: Both are valid identity-preserving transports. The difference is in phenomenology and risk profile, not fundamental ontology.
